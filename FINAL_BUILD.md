# Zahrat Elmadina Restaurant & Cafe — Final Build

Base: supplied restaurant website files.

## Included
- Customer ordering, cart, checkout and order tracking
- Arabic/English and RTL support
- Responsive customer navigation with 3-dash drawer
- Search and category filtering
- Admin food and category management
- Category enable/disable
- Product availability and stock controls
- Modifiers/add-ons foundation
- Reservations and table/NFC flow
- Loyalty points and flexible reward types
- Percentage/fixed discounts, free add-on, and free delivery rewards; free-meal rewards removed
- Offers/coupons
- Google Review CTA and testimonials
- Favorites and customer history lookups
- Kitchen and waiter views
- Receipt Designer
- Restaurant branding/settings
- Analytics and admin dashboard
- Zahrat Elmadina defaults for Avenue Mall, Obour City

## Important
This build preserves the existing Firebase configuration from the supplied files. Before real-world deployment, move the app to the restaurant's own Firebase project and implement production-grade authentication/authorization. See `PRODUCTION_SECURITY.md`.
